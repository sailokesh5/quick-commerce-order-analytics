# Sources

## City list and store counts
- Source: QCommerce Guide, "Blinkit Stores by City in India 2026 - Complete List"
- URL: https://qcommerceguide.com/blinkit-stores-by-city/
- Published: 14 Aug 2026. Accessed: 9 Oct 2026.
- Used for: the 20 cities and each city's store count.
- Notes: third-party site, not company data. It describes its figures as mapped stores from a dataset it does not fully document, and does not guarantee a location is currently active. I treat the counts as approximate and use them only to weight order volume and to group cities into store bands.
- The simulated company in this project is fictional. Its store network is modeled on this list.

## HRA city class (X / Y)
- Source: Government of India, Department of Expenditure, house rent allowance classification of cities (office memorandum dated 7 July 2017).
- I first read it through a republished copy: https://www.gconnect.in/orders-in-brief/allowance-orders-in-brief/house-rent-allowance-da.html
- Used for: the `hra_class` column. The classes are population-based and made for pay allowances. They are not a delivery-infrastructure measure.
- No single official "tier" list exists, so this is the closest official standard.
- Thane and Navi Mumbai are class X in `cities`. Where I found that listing: https://www.indianpaycalculator.in/city-classification.

## Everything else
Users, sessions, events, orders, times, amounts and cancellations are generated by `scripts/generate_data.py` (seed 42). The rates used in the generator are my own assumptions.
